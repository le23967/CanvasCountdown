**Canvas Countdown, now on Windows.** Your next Canvas deadline as a countdown
by the clock, with the same list, calendar and reminders as the Mac app.

This is a pre-release, for trying out before it becomes the main download.

## Which download

Both need Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11.

- **CanvasCountdown-Windows-1.0.0-x64-setup.exe**: most PCs (Intel and AMD).
- **CanvasCountdown-Windows-1.0.0-arm64-setup.exe**: ARM64 PCs (Snapdragon).

Not sure which you have? Open **Settings → System → About** and look at
**System type**.

## What it does

- Imports your Canvas calendar feed and keeps it up to date. You choose which
  events to import, and the feed link is stored in Windows Credential Manager.
- Shows the days left to your next deadline in the notification area, and on
  the taskbar button while the window is open, in colours and themes you can
  change.
- Lists what is coming up, with a calendar by day, week, month or year.
- Sends Windows notifications before each deadline, on a schedule you choose.
- Lets you add your own events, label events, filter or remove courses, and
  search everything. Anything you add can be undone.
- Reads deadlines from a screenshot with Windows' own text recognition. You
  check every row before anything is saved.
- Has an optional assistant that answers questions about your deadlines and
  turns a sentence into events for you to review. It runs on your PC or with
  an online service and API key of your choice.
- Installs updates itself, after checking the download is exactly the
  published file. If an update fails partway through, your previous version
  is restored.

## Installation

The installer isn't code-signed yet. If Windows shows **"Windows protected
your PC"**, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. It installs for your
account only, without asking for an administrator password.

Windows 11 hides new tray icons behind the **^** by the clock. Drag the
countdown onto the taskbar to keep it in view.

More detail, including where your data is kept and how to remove it, is in
[the Windows README](https://github.com/le23967/CanvasCountdown/blob/main/windows/README.md).
